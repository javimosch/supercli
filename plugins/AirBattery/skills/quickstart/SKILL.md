# AirBattery Plugin

Get the battery usage of all your devices on your Mac and put them on the Dock / Menu Bar / Widget! && 在Mac上获取你所有设备的电量信息并显示在Dock / 状态栏 / 小组件上!

## Commands

### General
- `AirBattery self version` — Print AirBattery version
- `AirBattery _ _` — Passthrough to airbattery CLI


- Get the battery usage of all your devices on your Mac and put them on the Dock / Menu Bar / Widget!
