# timelinize Plugin

Store your data from all your accounts and devices in a single cohesive timeline on your own computer

## Commands

### General
- `timelinize self version` — Print timelinize version
- `timelinize _ _` — Passthrough to timelinize CLI

## Usage Examples
- "Run timelinize"
- "Use timelinize to Store your data from all your accounts and devices in a sing"

## Installation

```bash
pip install timelinize
```

## Key Features
- Store your data from all your accounts and devices in a single cohesive timeline on your own compute
- CLI tool, headless and scriptable
