# better-shot Plugin

An open-source alternative to CleanShot X for macOS. Capture, edit, and enhance your screenshots with professional quality.

## Commands

### General
- `better-shot self version` — Print better-shot version
- `better-shot _ _` — Passthrough to better-shot CLI
